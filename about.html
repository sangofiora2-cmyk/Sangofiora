Our Story & Vision

Bringing Natural Serenity to Chennai & Homes Across India

Sango Fiora was born in Chennai out of a desire for effortless interior elegance. We recognized that live plants often struggle in air-conditioned apartments, busy offices, or humid coastal environments — requiring constant watering, sunlight alignment, and maintenance.

Our studio curates high-fidelity botanical reproductions built with real-touch foliage, botanical trunk textures, and weighted planters included. Each piece is designed to look vibrant and fresh every single day of the year, with zero effort required.
