About Sango Fiora

Sango Fiora was born in Chennai, Tamil Nadu, out of a passion for realistic interior greenery. We specialize in high-quality artificial plants and statement trees designed to look natural, feel close to live flora, and remain vibrant for years without watering, pruning, or sunlight.

Every piece in our collection is carefully curated with realistic leaf textures, natural stem details, and decorative pots included. Whether you are decorating a living room, setting up an office, styling a salon, or outfitting a commercial space, Sango Fiora brings everlasting greenery to your interiors.
