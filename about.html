Our Story

Crafting realistic, maintenance-free artificial plants and trees that bring natural warmth and architectural luxury to modern Indian spaces.

Sango Fiora was born out of a passion for realistic interior aesthetics. Based in Salem, Tamil Nadu, we specialize in high-quality artificial plants and trees designed to look natural, feel close to live greenery, and remain vibrant for years without any maintenance or watering.

Every piece in our collection is carefully handcrafted with high-grade foliage, realistic stem textures, and premium decorative pots included. Whether you are decorating a cosy living room, setting up an office space, styling a salon, or outfitting a retail store, Sango Fiora brings everlasting greenery to your interiors.
