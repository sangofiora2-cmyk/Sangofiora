Our Journey

Where Natural Botanical Beauty Meets Modern Convenience

Sango Fiora was founded in Salem, Tamil Nadu, with a clear mission: to eliminate the hassle of dying house plants while giving homeowners, designers, and business owners breathtaking greenery that looks and feels 100% natural.

Live plants require specific lighting, daily watering, pesticide care, and seasonal pruning. Our team hand-curates each botanical design with premium PE/silk foliage, realistic trunk veins, and weighted decorative ceramic/nursery pots — ensuring your space stays vibrant 365 days a year.
